# Event Report: "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

### Purpose of the Event

- Help students access AI and Cloud technologies that are being applied in the enterprise world.
- Learn how to build systems using **Agentic AI** to solve real-world problems.
- Learn from real experience in designing, deploying, and optimizing systems on the AWS platform.
- Practice problem-solving thinking, teamwork, and presenting technical ideas.

### Speakers

- **Nguyen Gia Hung** – Head of AWS Solutions Architecture, Vietnam.
- **Joseph Marazota** – Head of AWS Technology, ASEAN region.

### Featured Participating Teams

- **3KA**
- **One Team**
- **Plan V**
- **Signal Scout**
- **Six Pillars**

### Highlights

#### KFC Order-Assist Chatbot (Team One Team)

- Built a chatbot for placing orders directly on **Zalo** and **WhatsApp**, with no need to install an additional app.
- Includes an **order confirmation** step to reduce mistaken orders.
- Remembers customer history and preferences to personalize the experience.
- Optimized with **AWS Bedrock**, bringing the cost down to about **$0.006 USD per order**.

#### Competitor Analysis System (Team Signal Scout)

- Automatically collects data from financial reports, news, and various other sources.
- Applies an **Agent-to-Agent** model, in which multiple AI agents coordinate to perform different tasks.
- Built an in-house web browsing tool that reduced Cloud costs from **$94 USD to $35 USD per month**.
- Highlights the importance of cost and security optimization when deploying AI systems.

#### System Architecture Design Assistant (Team Plan V)

- Automatically generates system diagrams in **Draw.io** from natural-language requirements.
- Helps estimate AWS deployment costs.
- Uses **guardrails** to prevent the AI from adding unnecessary or high-cost services on its own.

#### Sheper Crowd Monitoring and Coordination System (Team 3KA)

- Uses smart cameras to monitor crowd density at airports or supermarkets.
- Processes video in real time and supports live zone-based people counting.
- Optimized the model from a large version down to **YOLOv8 Small**, significantly cutting costs while still meeting requirements.

#### Anti-Money-Laundering Investigation Support System (Team Six Pillars)

- Automatically analyzes suspicious transactions within a bank.
- Designed with a 3-layer architecture: **detection – evidence gathering – decision-making**.
- Incorporates mechanisms to limit AI hallucinations and strengthen data security.

### Key Takeaways

#### On Mindset

- **AI is a supporting tool** — humans remain the ones who make the final decisions.
- When building a product, priority should go to correctly solving the user's actual needs.
- Always stay proactive in learning and keeping up with new technologies to improve skills.

#### On Technical Aspects

- Understood how multiple **AI Agents** coordinate to solve a complex problem.
- Learned the importance of **guardrails** in limiting AI hallucinations.
- Learned how to design systems component by component for easier scaling and maintenance.

#### On Cloud and Cost Optimization

- Cloud costs can rise very quickly if the wrong model or service is chosen.
- It's better to choose a model that just meets the need rather than always defaulting to a large model.
- AWS offers many services that help optimize performance and cost when deploying AI systems.

### Applying This to My Studies

- Apply the knowledge gained about Agentic AI and AWS to coursework and projects.
- Learn more about Amazon Bedrock and other AWS AI services.
- When working in a team, divide tasks clearly and communicate more frequently.
- Prioritize building a basic version of the product first, then expand features afterward.
- Proactively join more workshops and hackathons to gain more hands-on experience.

### My Experience at the Event

Attending **FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week** was a very rewarding experience for me. The event gave me the opportunity to explore new Agentic AI technologies and AWS services through real business problems.

#### Learning from the Competing Teams

- The teams didn't just present their products — they also shared their build process, the difficulties they faced, and how they optimized their systems.
- I came to understand that beyond building a working product, optimizing performance and cost matters just as much.

#### Exploring New Technology

- I learned about Agentic AI and how multiple AI Agents coordinate to solve the same problem together.
- I also learned more about AWS AI services such as **Amazon Bedrock** and how they are applied in practice.

#### Learning from Real-World Experience

- Each team shared lessons learned during their experimentation, such as choosing the right AI model, optimizing Cloud costs, and limiting AI hallucinations.
- This helped me understand that building a real-world product requires balancing performance, cost, and user experience.

#### Motivation for Personal Growth

- The event gave me extra motivation to keep learning about AI and Cloud.
- I also hope to join more competitions and workshops to gain further hands-on experience and improve my own skills.
