In addition to my internship responsibilities, I had the opportunity to participate in **three professional events** organized by the AWS Community. These events not only provided valuable knowledge about Cloud Computing, AI, DevOps, and system architecture but also broadened my understanding of technology trends, career development, and professional working practices in real-world environments.


### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week (AABW) – Demo Day

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Highlights:** The presentation introduced several Agentic AI projects built on the AWS platform, combining services such as Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, SageMaker, and other technologies. These projects aim to solve real-world problems such as crowd management, AI assistants, intelligent ordering systems, and enterprise data analytics. Through the presentations, I gained a better understanding of the Multi-Agent model, how agents collaborate, and the process of building a complete AI application on a cloud platform.
