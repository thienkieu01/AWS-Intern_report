Over the course of my internship with the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, from **June 15, 2026** to **August 14, 2026**, I had the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a professional, project-based working environment. Throughout this period, I focused on studying and practicing the core services of Amazon Web Services (AWS) and applied this knowledge directly to building the **Library Management Website** project.

Taking on the lead role for the data side of the project, I was directly involved from the requirements-analysis stage, drafting the Proposal, designing the Use Case diagram, and building the data model (ERD), through to designing the overall system architecture, administering the **PostgreSQL** database, packaging the environment with **Docker**, and deploying the system to the AWS platform — including learning more about Amazon DynamoDB. Through this, I clearly strengthened professional skills such as database design, query optimization, data management, teamwork, progress coordination, and putting together technical documentation.

In terms of work attitude, I consistently maintained a serious approach, completed assigned tasks on schedule, followed the shared guidelines, and proactively engaged with my teammates to keep the work running efficiently.
