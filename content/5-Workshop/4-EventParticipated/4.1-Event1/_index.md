# Event Report (June 13, 2026)

### Event Objectives

The event was organized to help students and early-career engineers:

- Gain a deeper understanding of careers in Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), DevOps, and Data Analytics.
- Learn about recruitment processes, required technical skills, and career development pathways in the IT industry.
- Connect with the technology community through real-world experiences shared by engineers from technology companies and AWS Partners.
- Develop systems thinking, professional working culture, and problem-solving skills in enterprise environments.

### Speakers

- **Trong H. Truong** – DevOps Engineer @ Endava Vietnam.
- **Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị** – AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder, AWS Student Builder Group Leader.
- **Mr. Dat Pham** – Data Analytics Engineer with experience at Kamereo and Colgate-Palmolive.
- **Mr. Cường Nguyễn** – Process Engineer.

### Key Highlights

#### DevOps – Real-world Responsibilities and Career Opportunities

##### Understanding the DevOps Role

The presentation highlighted that DevOps is far more than building CI/CD pipelines or managing Docker and Kubernetes. It also involves solving operational challenges throughout the software development lifecycle.

Common DevOps responsibilities include:

- Troubleshooting testing and staging environments.
- Resolving system performance issues.
- Ensuring system security and reliability.
- Optimizing cloud infrastructure costs.
- Supporting continuous system operations.

##### Essential Knowledge and Skills

Core technical foundations include:

- Linux
- Networking
- Programming (Python, Golang)
- Git
- CI/CD
- Containers

In addition to technical expertise, DevOps engineers should develop:

- Systems thinking instead of focusing only on individual tasks.
- The ability to understand root causes before proposing solutions.
- Automation skills for repetitive tasks.
- Effective communication and cross-functional collaboration.

#### The Journey from Student to AWS Partner

The speaker shared an inspiring career roadmap from being a student to becoming an AWS professional:

1. Student Curiosity – Developing an interest in technology.
2. First Cloud Journey – Finding the right learning environment.
3. Workshop & Community – Learning through technology communities.
4. Hands-on Labs – Building practical experience through laboratory exercises.
5. School Projects – Applying knowledge to academic projects.
6. Portfolio – Creating a professional portfolio.
7. AWS Partner – Solving real business challenges.
8. Share Back – Giving back to the community through knowledge sharing.

The event also introduced several student development programs:

- AWS Student Builder Group
- AWS Cloud Clubs
- First Cloud AI Journey

These programs help students build a solid cloud foundation through topics such as:

- AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (VPC)
- Amazon EC2
- Cost Management
- Observability

#### Real-world Experience of a Data Analytics Engineer

##### Data Analytics in Business

The speakers explained how Data Analytics is applied across different industries.

**E-commerce & Logistics**

- Building periodic business reports.
- Designing dashboards for trend monitoring.
- Analyzing revenue and operational costs.

**Manufacturing & FMCG**

- Analyzing IoT-generated data.
- Optimizing manufacturing costs.
- Supporting digital transformation initiatives.

##### Essential Skills

A successful Data Analytics Engineer should develop:

- Critical Thinking
- Communication
- Data Storytelling
- Problem Solving

##### Career Development Model

The proposed career roadmap consists of five stages:

- **Follower:** Executes assigned tasks under guidance.
- **Learner:** Takes initiative to learn and ask meaningful questions.
- **Problem Solver:** Analyzes problems and proposes effective solutions.
- **System Thinker:** Understands the bigger picture and system-wide impacts.
- **Super Star:** Leads strategic initiatives and mentors future professionals.

#### Recruitment Process and Corporate Culture

##### Recruitment Process in Multinational Companies

A typical recruitment process consists of four stages:

1. **Screening & Initial Interview**

- Resume screening using ATS.
- Initial interview conducted in English.

2. **Technical Assessment**

- Algorithm and programming tests.
- Problem-solving assessment.

3. **Technical Interview**

- Technical discussion.
- Real-world scenario analysis.

4. **Culture Fit Interview**

- Evaluation of alignment with the company's culture and core values.

##### Workplace Culture

Two important organizational cultures were highlighted.

**No-Blame Post-Mortem**

- Focus on identifying system issues instead of blaming individuals after incidents.

**Caring & Inclusive**

- Respect diversity.
- Build a people-centered working environment.
