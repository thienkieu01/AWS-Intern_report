> In this section, I would like to share my reflections after participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program. The feedback below is provided with the hope of helping the program continue to improve and deliver even greater value to future participants.

## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

The internship environment in the program is extremely dynamic and comfortable. The seniors and fellow students at FCAJ are enthusiastic in sharing ideas and readily provide support to resolve technical difficulties throughout the project implementation. The modern infrastructure and workspace create a comfortable atmosphere and help optimize student concentration.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

The mentors provided very close guidance, offering accurate technical direction and suggesting solutions instead of giving direct answers, helping me improve my independent problem-solving skills. The admin team always provided timely support regarding AWS accounts, administrative procedures, and documentation, creating the most favorable conditions for my learning and work.

### 3. Relevance to My Major

The assigned tasks closely aligned with my Computer Science major, particularly in the areas of Database Design and AWS Cloud Computing. Working on this real-world project helped me solidify the theoretical knowledge I gained in university and understand how to apply it to a practical business product model.

### 4. Learning and Development Opportunities

The internship gave me the opportunity to access many advanced technologies such as AWS RDS, VPC, Docker and professional database management processes. Besides professional capacity, I also practiced Agile/Sprint project implementation thinking, team coordination skills and methodical packaging of technical documents.

### 5. Teamwork

The work culture is very open, respects individual creativity, and emphasizes teamwork. During peak project phases (Sprint system development and testing), everyone proactively collaborates, shares pressure, and supports each other to ensure the overall quality and progress of the entire team.

### 6. Training Program

The program provides full access to AWS Cloud services and offers flexible internship schedules for students. Internal professional development sessions (Workshops/Immersion Days) are a major plus, helping interns broaden their practical perspectives.

## Additional Feedback

### What I Appreciated Most

What I'm most proud of is being able to directly design and standardize the database and bring that infrastructure to life on the AWS Cloud environment. Seeing the product built by the team run smoothly is an incredibly valuable experience.

### Areas for Improvement

The program could include additional comprehensive architecture review sessions between teams during the mid-term internship, allowing interns from different fields (Dev, Data, DevOps) to learn how to solve each other's problems.

### Would I Recommend This Program?

Absolutely. This is an ideal environment for those who want to gain practical experience with AWS Cloud, hone their teamwork skills, and enhance their professional data design/management thinking.

## Suggestions and Expectations

- **Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?** 
The program could be expanded to include more advanced topics such as Cloud Cost Optimization or database CI/CD workflow automation to provide students with more practical experience.


- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**
I am very much looking forward to the opportunity to continue participating in or collaborating on further in-depth programs/projects implemented by the company and organization.

- **Any other comments (free sharing):**

I sincerely thank the organizers of the First Cloud AI Journey program, the mentors, and the admin team for creating such a meaningful internship experience, which has helped me grow significantly in both professional competence and practical work skills.
