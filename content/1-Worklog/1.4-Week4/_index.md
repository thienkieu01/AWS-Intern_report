* **Knowledge gained:**
  * Learned advanced PostgreSQL concepts such as Partitioning and Indexing, and understood the purpose of these techniques in database optimization.

  * Gained knowledge of Data Lakes and how to manage data across stages using Lifecycle Rules.

* **Contribution to the project:**
  * Participated in analyzing the system's functionalities from a data-processing perspective and identified the CRUD operations for each functionality.
